# World & Navigation

## Spatial Navigation

Status: Future

Repairs should occur at physical station locations instead of launching directly into puzzles.

Goals

- Strengthen station identity.
- Create anticipation before repairs.
- Make subsystems memorable.
- Reinforce environmental storytelling.

Possible implementations

- Station map
- Door transitions
- Corridor cards
- Room hero scenes
- Equipment bays
- Airlocks
- Maintenance tunnels

References

- 2001: A Space Odyssey
- Alien
- The Andromeda Strain
- The Last of Us

## Layered Alarm States

Status: Future

Each subsystem contributes independent audio based on its state.

Ideas

Armed pulse continues during alarms.
Reactor hum changes with load.
Ventilation remains audible unless it actually fails.
Relay clicks continue during emergencies.
New alerts layer onto existing sounds rather than replacing them.

Reference

2001: A Space Odyssey
