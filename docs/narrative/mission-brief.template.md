## Significant clues and records

For each significant clue, provide an ID, source, author/originating system, creation time or relative order, source belief at creation, literal information, intended inference, certainty/ambiguity, prerequisite context and possible later reinterpretation. Attribute later status/history separately from the original author's words.

## Readiness

List unresolved prerequisites explicitly. Full preparation requires implementation-ready decisions/copy before the main planner returns IMPLEMENT. A draft may identify a dependency; a final implementation plan may not retain meaningful unresolved placeholders. Preserve links to approved puzzle design and the specific observable behavior the artifact contextualizes.
